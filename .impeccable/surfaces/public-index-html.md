## Scope and visitor mode

- Surface: `public/index.html`
- Mode: Operate

## Audience and job

Policy and technical-governance researchers arrive to configure a compute-reallocation scenario, compare trajectories, and inspect the assumptions behind the comparison.

## Direction

The surface is a flight-test telemetry notebook: a cool-white control rack and synchronized plots make the calculator itself the first-view thesis. Its memorable moment is a sweep line and trace redraw on entry and discrete scenario changes; continuous slider movement updates both trajectories immediately without replaying the sequence.

## Constraints

- Keep the existing `runModel(assumptions)` handoff boundary intact.
- Label all interim chart geometry as illustrative, never as a model result.
- Support keyboard input, reduced motion, and mobile widths without horizontal page overflow.
- Do not reintroduce the discarded flip-flop or mascot concept.

## Unresolved
