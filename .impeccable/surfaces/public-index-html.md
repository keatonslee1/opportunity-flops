## Scope and visitor mode

- Surface: `public/index.html`
- Mode: Operate

## Audience and job

Policy and technical-governance researchers arrive to configure a compute-reallocation scenario, compare trajectories, and inspect the assumptions behind the comparison.

## Direction

The surface is a scientific monograph: ivory stock, printer's ink, and six numbered figure plates inset on cool-white paper beside an ivory margin of controls. Serif display and mathematics against a humanist sans body. Its memorable moment is the sweep line and trace redraw on entry and discrete scenario changes; continuous slider movement updates all six plots immediately without replaying the sequence.

The methodology write-up now lives at `/methodology`, so this surface is the instrument alone.

## Constraints

- Keep the existing `runModel(assumptions)` handoff boundary intact.
- The six chart plates are frozen: their geometry, encoding, animation, and the seven tokens their strokes read must not change. Figure plates stay near-white, or the frozen traces shift in apparent contrast.
- Present chart geometry as modeled sensitivity output, never as an empirical estimate or absolute forecast.
- Keep the active β, γ, δ, and λ assumptions visible and identify the normalized software and risk baselines.
- Keep the 10,000-draw protocol, triangular inputs, fixed seed, and P10/P50/P90 bundle interpretation inspectable; identify them as working assumptions rather than empirical estimates or joint outcome percentiles.
- Support keyboard input, reduced motion, and mobile widths without horizontal page overflow. Navigation wraps at mobile widths; it is never hidden.
- No drop shadows and no cards. Depth comes from the ivory/white surface change and from rule weight.
- Do not assert liveness the system does not have; the model is synchronous arithmetic and is never "online".
- Do not reintroduce the discarded flip-flop or mascot concept.
- Label the constant `R_B(t) = 100` path and the λ triangular input as working sensitivity assumptions because Parts IV–V do not supply them.

## Unresolved

Empirical distribution calibration, propagated joint-output uncertainty intervals, time-varying R&D compute, a user-selectable horizon, and capability-threshold delay remain future model extensions.
