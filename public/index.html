The model is simple on purpose

It connects one policy choice to two modeled consequences. The equations are implemented as written; z selects a reproducible parameter bundle calibrated by Monte Carlo from explicit, non-empirical distribution assumptions.

  1. AllocatePolicy compute is CRP = (1 − x)CR. Training compute stays unchanged.
  2. PropagateSoftware follows dS/dt = A CRβSγ.
  3. TranslateCapability follows M = CTαSδ, so the training-compute term cancels in the policy comparison.
  4. CompareCapability gap = 1 − (SP/SB)δ.

The bundles rank-align each parameter's marginal percentile. They are sensitivity cases, not joint outcome percentiles or empirical estimates. Seeded calibration makes the same z selection reproduce the same parameter set on every run.

Software begins at index 100. The baseline is normalized to index 200 in 2034, so the charts communicate policy divergence—not a dated forecast of absolute capability. The normalization absorbs A; unchanged training compute makes α cancel from relative outputs.
